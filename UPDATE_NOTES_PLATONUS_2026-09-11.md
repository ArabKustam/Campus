# Platonus and direct assistant improvements — 2026-09-11

- UMKD courses expose their published requirements as Campus document buttons. A per-account authenticated Worker route retrieves PDF documents through the encrypted Platonus session and serves them from the Campus origin, without forwarding credentials or upstream cookies/redirects. Course IDs must exist in the account's latest UMKD snapshot, file IDs must belong to its requirements. PDF signature verified; maximum 20 MiB. No persistent PDF cache: the university must be available when retrieving a document.
- Verified on the signed-in user's Campus UI: Ecology syllabus opens in the embedded PDF viewer, 12 pages, Campus URL. Other available sections are listed. Files can open in a new Campus tab or download.
- Schedule preview is grouped into weekday cards with two-week selection, per-lesson selection, conflict labels and a single import-selected button. Imports retain existing optimistic revision checks and individual audit/rollback entries. On a partial failure, the UI reports the completed count and refreshes; it does not retry applied rows. No real user schedule changes were applied during QA.
- Direct owner cancellations resolve explicit dates, numbered pairs, times, current/next references and subjects against actual occurrences. No explicit subject is required when an explicit pair locator uniquely identifies the lesson. Missing dates or ambiguous targets yield actionable questions. Negation and conditional statements are not auto-cancelled. Russian grammatical cases now work with full single-word subject names even without a short name.
- Regression checks cover PDF authorization and content validation; direct cancellation ambiguity, negation, moved dates; end-to-end assistant cancellation and rollback; inflected course names; existing cloud Platonus and import checks. Worker typecheck and frontend production build passed.

Remaining known limitation: journal grade schema is still unsupported. It is independent of these three changes. The user's exact previously failing cancellation phrase was requested but has not been provided at this point.
